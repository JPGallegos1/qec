# Que Estan Construyendo

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7 with Vite 8, Tailwind CSS 4, pnpm, Resend, PostHog, and React Email. Editions live in local Markdown files. The MVP does not use a CMS, database, user accounts, or an admin panel.

## Users

The primary audience is founders and startup operators in Argentina who need a fast, reliable view of what peers are building. Secondary readers include investors, developers, makers, and other active participants in the Argentine startup ecosystem.

## Product Purpose

Every 15 days, Que Estan Construyendo sends a short, manually curated edition about concrete work by startups, founders, and indie hackers connected to Argentina. A reader should understand the essential signals in about five minutes without reviewing multiple social networks, communities, videos, and podcasts.

Initial success means publishing three editions over six weeks, reaching at least 30 relevant subscribers, gaining readers beyond the editor's direct circle, receiving at least five replies, recommendations, testimonials, or story submissions, and learning which sections repeatedly earn engagement.

## Positioning

QEC is selective rather than exhaustive. Every included item is recent, tied to Argentina, grounded in an original source, and selected for a concrete launch, advance, event, or useful operating insight. The product adds manual verification and editorial judgment instead of automatically aggregating or summarizing feeds.

## Operating Context

The editor gathers sources over each fortnight, receives community submissions, processes sources and transcripts in NotebookLM, removes duplicates, drafts summaries, verifies dates and claims manually, selects no more than 10 to 12 items, publishes the web edition, sends the email, and reviews metrics seven days later. The target is less than six hours of editorial work per issue.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A landing page with the value proposition, fortnightly frequency, subscription form, latest issue example, story-submission CTA, issue archive, privacy notice, and consent.
- One responsive newsletter template with visible Reply-To, required unsubscribe handling, measurable links, and one subscriber list.
- Optional issue sections: introduction, up to five startup stories, up to three indie projects, up to three videos or podcasts, radar links, participation, and one feedback question.
- Resend owns contacts, sends, deliverability metrics, clicks, and replies. PostHog owns web acquisition and conversion events.
- Community submissions are emailed to the editor; there is no editorial dashboard.
- A single local Markdown source is the desired future source for web and email, but that automation is deferred until the dominant manual bottleneck is known.
- Collection automation, unreviewed summaries, apps, accounts, personalization, referrals, sponsor portals, and advanced commercial tooling are outside the MVP.

## Brand Commitments

The public name is "Que Estan Construyendo"; QEC is its operational abbreviation. Product language is Argentine Spanish and uses direct local forms such as "envia" and "contanos". The voice is concise, specific, skeptical of hype, respectful of readers' time, and explicit when content is sponsored.

The visual identity is pinned to a warm independent-publication system: paper `#FAF6EF`, ink `#1C1815`, orange `#E5571F`, Newsreader for editorial text, Hanken Grotesk for interface text, and JetBrains Mono for dates and sources. The public wordmark combines the full name with an orange QEC sigla.

Sponsorship is deferred while QEC builds its initial community. The public site and edition-zero email do not include sponsor offers or commercial calls to action.

## Product Principles

- Save attention through aggressive editorial selection.
- Prefer original sources, concrete progress, and verified context over promotion.
- Keep the product operationally manual until repeated work identifies the right automation.
- Label demonstrations and sponsorships honestly; never fabricate proof.
- Measure recurring clicks, replies, contributions, and recommendations above unreliable open rates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public web and email surfaces must be responsive, keyboard accessible, readable without motion, and maintain clear consent, privacy, sponsorship, and unsubscribe language.
